## 2. Asset-by-Asset Design Breakdown

### A. The Laptop & Built-in Keyboard
- **Reference Look:** Think a clean, stylized MacBook Pro or ThinkPad drawn in 2D comic inking (like *Dark Dome's* computers or *Rusty Lake: The White Door* terminal).
- **Linework:** Heavy 2.5px black ink border defining the lid and chassis.
- **Coloring:** Matte dark slate-gray (`#2a3547`) with a subtle lighter rim highlight on the top bezel. Flat square keys separated by clean dark lines.
- **Lighting Effect:** A faint cool cyan-white glow radiates from the screen, softly highlighting the top row of keys and trackpad.

### B. The Laptop Screen & Browser Tabs
- **Reference Look:** A vintage or indie point-and-click desktop OS (like *Hypnospace Outlaw* meets clean Dark Dome UI).
- **Tabs Styling:** 
  - Instead of standard Chrome tabs, use **hand-drawn folder tabs** with slightly rounded tops, bold 2px ink borders, and small clean icons (`📄 Slides`, `📋 Specs`, `💬 Reddit ✕`, `▶ Lo-Fi ✕`, `🗨 Chat ✕`).
  - Active tab: `#162947` with a bright blue indicator line and white text.
  - Distractor tabs: Slightly muted slate with a crisp red `✕` hover button.
- **Window Body:** Dark navy background with high-contrast, easily readable white and golden text. Lecture slides look like clean university PDFs; distraction tabs have authentic recognizable touches (orange Reddit alien icon, red YouTube progress bar, purple Discord hash symbol).

### C. The Grounding Journal / Diary (Left Desk)
- **Reference Look:** A worn, hardbound midnight-blue or deep espresso-leather field notebook with aged brass corner protectors and a ribbon bookmark.
- **Exterior Art:** 
  - Subtle gold-foil embossing: `"JOURNAL"` or an abstract spiral compass on the cover.
  - A small pen resting beside it at a natural angle.
- **Interior Open View:**
  - Off-white aged parchment (`#f8f4e8` or dark night-mode parchment `#1a2638`).
  - Raw handwritten cursive in dark ink (`Caveat` font).
  - Clean hover interaction: when cursor hovers over the anxious thought, the handwriting gently fades out and reveals centered, clean gray text explaining the clinical perfectionism trap beneath it.

### D. The Task List / Notepad (Right Desk)
- **Reference Look:** A standard vertical tear-off legal pad or spiral pocket notepad.
- **Exterior Art:**
  - Creamy lined paper with faint blue horizontal ruling and a red left margin line.
  - A dark pencil or pen resting beside it.
- **Checklist Visuals:**
  - Checkboxes drawn as clean square ink outlines.
  - Shrunk tasks highlight in calm mint-green (`#86efac`) with a satisfying subtle pulse animation.

### E. The Sticky Note on the Wall (Right)
- **Reference Look:** Classic 3x3 square canary yellow Post-It note with a single dark brass thumbtack or pushpin at the top.
- **Angle & Shadow:** Tilted by just +1° to feel organic; soft drop shadow grounding it against the blue wall.
- **Typography:** Bold hand-scrawled black ink (`"DON'T MESS THIS UP"`).
- **End-Game Transformation:** When self-compassion is achieved, the paper smoothly morphs into soft soothing sage-green reading: `"DON'T BE SO HARD ON YOURSELF"`.

### F. Potted Plant in the Background (Left Shelf)
- **Reference Look:** A small ceramic terracotta or matte navy pot with a snake plant (*Sansevieria*) or a leafy pothos.
- **Role in Scene:** 
  - Plants in Dark Dome games provide organic softness against cold architectural lines.
  - In our game, the plant subtly catches the warm yellow cone of the desk lamp, adding visual depth and life to an otherwise sterile late-night study room.

## 4. Key Questions to Dial In Your Vision

1. **Title Screen Art:** Would you like the title screen to stay with the big rainy glass window, or would you like a silhouette of the character looking out at the city lights?
2. **Rain Intensity Control:** Should the rain outside get harder when internal panic rises (e.g. during task paralysis) and calm down to a gentle mist after the breathing exercise?
3. **Desk Lighting Color:** We've added the warm golden lamp light (`#ffda8c`). Do you prefer this cozy warm amber glow, or would you like a warmer orange candle-like tone?
4. **Custom Audio File:** Would you like to use the built-in procedural rain audio (zero file download needed), or would you like help placing an exact recorded `.mp3` file into your folder?
