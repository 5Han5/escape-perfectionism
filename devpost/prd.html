Behaviors and Checks

What "working" means, screen by screen

Paralysis and the three choices
  • Thoughts appear in order: which first, am I even going to finish, maybe the other one.
  • Three choices: check the phone, what's more fun, just pick anything.
  • Phone or fun adds time pressure and a gloomier screen.
  • "Just pick anything" starts the puzzles.
Shrink the task puzzle

"Finish 2 chapters" becomes "Finish studying the first 3 pages of chapter 1", then "Open the book to page 1."

  • The text steps down in three stages and ends on the tiny first action.
Close the tabs puzzle

The view zooms into the laptop. The player closes distracting tabs and keeps the lecture slides and the assignment.

  • Only the lecture slides and assignment tabs remain open.
Journal

Found in the drawer, placed on the desk, offered as a choice when feelings build, and ends with a last page: notice the feeling and why, calm down and be kind to yourself, set the next step.

  • The drawer opens, the journal lands on the desk.
  • "Go to your journal" appears when feelings build.
  • Each entry explains the reason behind a feeling.
  • The last page lists the three steps.
Quiet the chest tightness

A hold-to-breathe interaction settles the screen before the ending.

  • Holding the control calms the visuals; releasing early lets them tighten again.
States
  • First use: intro plays, no journal yet.
  • Journal not found: the journal choice does not appear (assumption: a gentle nudge toward the drawer).
  • Wasting time: clock and deadline note feel closer, but the game never ends in failure.
  • Persistence: nothing saved; refresh starts over.
