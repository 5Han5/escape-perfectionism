Escape Perfectionism

About the Game

Escape Perfectionism is a short story game about getting stuck because everything has to be perfect.

You sit at a student's desk late at night. Step by step, we will shrink a big to-do list, clear the laptop, read your journal, and take a slow breath. Then you start. No pressure. No perfect.

  • 🕒 About 5 minutes
  • 🖱️ Mouse only: click things on the desk
  • 🎧 Sound is recommended
  • 💙 Note: this game talks about stress and self-criticism, gently
